5. Gram stain colour comparator

Purple / Violet

Compatible with Gram-positive staining

Gram-positive organisms generally retain the crystal violet–iodine complex.

Pink / Red

Compatible with Gram-negative staining

Gram-negative organisms generally take up counterstain after decolorization.

Mixed / Uncertain

Interpret with caution

Review stain quality, decolorization, smear thickness and image quality.

7. Educational report

8. Learn Gram staining

1 Prepare smear
2 Air dry
3 Heat fix
4 Crystal violet
5 Gram iodine
6 Decolorizer
7 Counterstain
8 Oil immersion

Gram-positive bacteria generally appear purple/violet, while Gram-negative bacteria generally appear pink/red after staining. Actual interpretation depends on proper technique and microscopy.

9. Common causes of misleading appearance

  • Thick or uneven smear
  • Over-decolorization
  • Under-decolorization
  • Old culture
  • Incorrect staining time
  • Poor reagent quality
  • Inadequate washing
  • Excessive heat fixation
  • Poor illumination
  • Camera exposure / white balance
